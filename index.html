05 / Frequently asked

Before you download.

Can I use the assets in commercial games?

Commercial use is subject to each pack's included license. The paid Dark Fantasy Heroines collection includes a commercial game asset license; its free demo is for noncommercial evaluation.

Do all packs contain editable SVGs?

No. The Dark Fantasy RPG UI Kit includes editable SVGs; the RPG Icons pack includes PNGs and JSON atlas data. The new Heroines pack provides transparent PNG artwork and a Godot showcase.

Can I use the artwork in Godot or Unity?

PNG artwork can be used in compatible engines. Godot is optional for the character artwork; the included interactive showcase is a Godot project.

Where can I download a free sample?

Open the new Heroines listing and select Download Demo. Other packs may also offer free samples.

Where do I buy and download assets?

Purchases and downloads are handled through the official itch.io store. This website is a product guide, not a payment processor.

Are the assets AI-assisted?

Asset creation involves AI-assisted artwork workflows. See the AI disclosure and license on each product listing.
