Frequently asked questions

Are spritesheets included?

Yes. In addition to the 480 separate icon PNG files, buyers receive a second ZIP with transparent spritesheets and matching JSON atlas metadata.

Do I get editable SVG or PSD source files?

No. The icon files are provided as transparent PNG images, plus PNG spritesheets and JSON metadata.

Does the icon pack include a ready-made Godot or Unity project?

No. These are importable artwork assets, not complete game-engine projects or gameplay systems.
